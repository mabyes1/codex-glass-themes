## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: static background texture for a frosted-glass desktop aquarium theme, wide 16:9 landscape. Create an exquisitely atmospheric, mysterious deep ocean scene viewed through gently frosted glass. Cinematic painterly realism, restrained midnight navy, ink blue and subtle cold cyan. Vast dark open water dominates the center 70 percent, giving plenty of quiet negative space behind white UI text. A faint source of light far above sends delicate volumetric shafts diagonally down from the upper center-left, dissolving into blue-black depth. Very distant irregular submerged rock walls and sparse delicate reef silhouettes are confined to the far side edges and lowest corners, barely emerging from mist; a sense of enormous depth and stillness. Tiny sparse suspended marine particles, no bright pinprick stars. Refined layered haze, soft edges, subtle photographic diffusion, softly out of focus but with beautiful depth and tonal variation. The upper light should be subdued, never white or glaring. Entire composition quite dark yet rich, bottom fades toward near-black indigo. This is an environment plate: no fish, whales, jellyfish, large bubbles, people, divers, submarines, windows, aquarium frames, text, interface, logos or watermark. Moving fish and bubbles will be composited separately later. Do not make it a colorful tropical aquarium or a flat gradient. Elegant, moody, mysterious, quiet luxury.
