# A workspace that feels like yours.

Soft glass. A little color. A whole ocean of ideas.

Codex Glass Themes brings your own atmosphere to the app you already use.
Keep the conversation readable, choose a background, and get back to building.

## Find your focus

- **Aquarium** — a quiet deep sea with moving fish and four scenes.
- **Cosmos** — the Milky Way, drifting dust, and passing comets.
- **Colors & images** — familiar controls for a more personal workspace.

> Your text stays clear. Your background gets a little more interesting.

const workspace = {

Install once. Open Codex from its usual icon. Make yourself at home.
